## About This Project

The RE-TRUE project was a comprehensive branding and design initiative for a university intramurals event that pushed the boundaries of creative expression through pixel art and retro aesthetics. This project represented a unique challenge to blend nostalgic 8-bit visual elements with modern design principles.

### Design Philosophy

The core concept revolved around creating a cohesive visual identity that would resonate with the student community while standing out from typical university event branding. The pixel art theme was chosen to evoke feelings of nostalgia and gaming culture, which are deeply embedded in the target demographic.

### Key Achievements

- **Character Design**: Created custom sprite characters that became the mascots of the event, each with unique animations and personality traits
- **Typography System**: Developed a custom 8-bit style typography that maintained readability while capturing the retro aesthetic
- **Motion Graphics**: Produced animated sequences that brought static designs to life, including character animations and promotional videos
- **Music Production**: Composed original chiptune-style music that complemented the visual design and enhanced the overall brand experience

### Technical Implementation

The project utilized a combination of traditional graphic design tools and specialized pixel art software to achieve the desired aesthetic. Each element was carefully crafted to ensure consistency across all touchpoints, from digital publications to social media assets.

### Impact and Results

The RE-TRUE branding successfully energized the student community, with high engagement rates on social media platforms and positive feedback from event participants. The project demonstrated how creative design can transform a simple university event into a memorable cultural experience.

This project showcases expertise in graphic design, digital publication, character design, character animation, pixel art, and music production, demonstrating the ability to deliver high-quality logo suites, brand guidelines, social media assets, motion graphic animations, music production, custom sprite character animations, GIFs, and social media camera filters that meet client objectives and industry standards.
